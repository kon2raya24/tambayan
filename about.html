About Tambayan

Free Pinoy games you can play in any browser

What this is

A tambayan is the place where people hang out: the sari-sari store bench, the corner under the mango tree, the waiting shed where the barkada (group of friends) passes the afternoon. Tambayan Arcade is meant to be that kind of place for games. Every game here is free, runs in a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and needs no download or account.

The games take classic arcade and board game ideas and set them in the Philippines. The maze chase happens in a palengke (wet market). The falling-blocks puzzle is a construction site. Snakes and Ladders climbs a barrio to the town fiesta. Others start from things that are already ours: tumbang preso and other street games, the sungka board, and the aswang, tiyanak and kapre of our folklore.

Who makes the games

I'm Lemmuel Turaya, a developer from the Philippines. I build these games one at a time, on my own, and add each one here when it's ready to play. Some are small and quick, like the daily sungka puzzle; others have story chapters, unlockable characters and two-player modes. You can see more of my work on my portfolio.

How they're made

The games are written in plain JavaScript and run on the web platform itself, with the 3D games drawn using Three.js. Some 3D characters are animated with Mixamo motion clips, and some scenes use free textures and environments from Poly Haven.

Each game has its own guide on this site explaining the rules, the controls, a few tips, and the Filipino tradition or story behind it, so players who didn't grow up with tumbang preso or the legend of the Bakunawa can learn where the game comes from.
